How to read this

  • Images are 10 m Sentinel-2 pixels. Weed is measured as the covered share of each pixel, so small mats still count.
  • A drop can also come from a mat drifting downstream. A matching rise on the next stretch down points to drift rather than removal.
  • Cloudy, hazy or monsoon images are left out. Low-confidence stretches (narrow channel, many bank pixels) are excluded here.
  • Figures are checked monthly against Landsat 8/9 and yearly against weed patches planted into real images.
