    Cross-checked by other satellites

    Two independent satellites measure the same river. They confirm whether weed is rising or falling; neither is sharp enough to confirm exact levels.

    Same direction: whenever Sentinel-2 showed a change of 5 points or more between two compared days, how often the other satellite moved the same way. Landsat is re-checked on the 1st of every month; the radar is re-calibrated with every update.
